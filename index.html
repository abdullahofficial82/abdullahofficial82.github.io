Research

L-Asparaginase from Saprolegnia parasitica: Identification, Structural and Functional Insights

MPhil Thesis · University of the Punjab · Feb 2026 – Jan 2027

Starting from a novel gene, characterizing the enzyme in silico (structure prediction, docking and phylogenetics), then validating it at the bench: RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, gene-specific primer design and PCR, cloning into pET-28a, screening of positive E. coli DH5α clones, expression in E. coli BL21 and SDS-PAGE confirmation of the predicted protein. Supervised by Dr. M. Akhtar Ali.

Enzymes Structure Prediction Docking Phylogenetics Cloning Protein Expression

Dengue Virus Genomics — Genome Mapping & Evolutionary Classification

MPhil Research · University of the Punjab · 2025 – Present

Genome mapping and phylogenetic classification of dengue virus genomes to study viral evolution and strain diversity. Supervised by Dr. M. Akhtar Ali.

Genomics Phylogenetics Dengue Virus

Antiviral Drug Design via CADD against MERS-CoV & RSV

Final Year Project · COMSATS University Islamabad · Feb – Aug 2023

Funded by the National Grassroots ICT Research Initiative (NGIRI). Supervised by Dr. Qudsia Yousafi and co-supervised by Mr. Muhammad Saad Khan.

CADD Drug Design MERS-CoV RSV Molecular Docking

NGS Variant Analysis Workflow in Linux

BIRL, LUMS · Oct 2023 – Jun 2024

Deployed a Next-Generation Sequencing workflow in the Linux environment: read quality control (FastQC, MultiQC), alignment (BWA), SAM/BAM processing (SAMtools) and variant calling and annotation (GATK), with scripts to streamline preprocessing.

NGS Linux GATK Variant Calling
